# Rush & Risk — TestFlight Beta 1

Version 1.0 (Build 2)

## Beta description

Rush & Risk is a fast Detroit-inspired arcade game about catching falling records while a mischievous rival changes the rhythm. This first beta includes 20 levels, touch and drag controls, wind gusts, record-stealing birds, late-level earthquakes, reactive character animation, local scores and progression, earned power-ups, and responsive iPhone/iPad layouts.

## What to test

- Play Levels 1–12 and report whether the revised late-level targets feel challenging without becoming exhausting.
- Confirm Shield appears after 10 catches, absorbs one miss without costing a life, and then disappears.
- Confirm Slow Motion appears after 15 catches, is clearly labeled, and lasts approximately five seconds.
- Test landscape-left and landscape-right on iPhone, including rapid tap and drag movement.
- On iPad, check landscape layout, HUD spacing, catcher readability, pause, and resizing where available.
- Watch bird record theft and release: the record should stay visible and reachable.
- Verify pause/resume, level transitions, background/foreground, game over, restart, and local progress.
- Audio begins muted. At low device volume, explicitly enable it in Settings and compare the slower menu loop with the faster gameplay loop. Report audibility, route used (speaker/headphones/Bluetooth), and any silence or distortion.

## Known beta limitation

Bundled normalized music replaces the previous runtime-generated bass-heavy loops, but physical-device audibility has not yet been verified. Automated validation remains permanently silent.

## Feedback requested

Include device model, iOS version, level, orientation, reproduction steps, and a screenshot or screen recording when practical. The owner's existing gameplay strategy is intentionally preserved for this beta and should not be treated as a defect without separate approval.
